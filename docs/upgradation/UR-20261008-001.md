## 2. Purpose

Close the residual items an independent audit of the `docs/` corpus surfaced, and move the corpus from **machine-verified** to **human-attested** without weakening the structural guarantees the 25 gates already provide.

The corpus is internally consistent today; this request is about the distance between "internally consistent" and "a person has read and stands behind this."

---

## 3. Problem Statement

The audit established that the corpus is structurally sound and gate-green, but carries a set of tracked residuals that no gate can close on its own:

1. Every artifact that declares a review state reads as pending, with no verifying commit recorded — so the corpus is machine-verified, not human-attested.
2. Revision stamps disagree across the manifest, the change log, and the engine documents.
3. Verification documents cite different commits for the same class of claim.
4. The module-manifest contract still lists code gaps while the reconciliation ledger reports none.
5. Coverage is enforced as a blended average, contrary to the project's own per-layer rule.
6. Four verification surfaces were never executed for want of live infrastructure.
7. The change log and the repository status file are partly unpopulated.

Each is recorded honestly in the corpus. The purpose of this request is to schedule and discharge them.

---

## 4. Scope

**In scope.** The reader-facing corpus and its machine-readable ledgers: `blueprint/`, `documentation/`, `spec/`, `contracts/`, `status/`, `adr/`, `upgradation/`, and the front-matter contract that governs them.

**Out of scope.** Application source behaviour (`apps/main/`), module packages, deployment topology, and any feature work. This request changes documentation and its ledgers only. Deferred capabilities and deferred greenfield items remain out of scope and MUST NOT be smuggled in as changes.

## 6. Requested Changes

Each request carries a statement, a rationale, an owning target, and its own acceptance test.

### RQ-01 — Human-attestation pass (closes F-06)
**Statement.** Every reader-facing artifact SHALL be reviewed by a named owner and SHALL have its review state moved from pending to verified with the verifying commit recorded, in the order the front-matter contract defines.
**Rationale.** Structural gates cannot attest correctness of a sentence; only a person can. This is the corpus's own stated boundary.
**Target.** All reader-facing artifacts, beginning with `blueprint/system-map.md`, `spec/request-layer/`, and `spec/modules/`; the review-state fields defined by the front-matter contract.
**Acceptance.** A sampled section can be traced to a named reviewer and a commit; no artifact that declares a review state remains pending at close.

### RQ-02 — Per-layer coverage conformance (closes F-10)
**Statement.** The coverage configuration SHALL express the per-layer floors the project mandates and SHALL NOT rely on a single blended average; the untested bootstrap entry SHALL be brought under test or explicitly exempted with a recorded reason.
**Rationale.** A blended threshold can pass while a layer sits far below its floor.
**Target.** `spec/lifecycle/testing-architecture-qa.md` (the owning section for the test architecture).
**Acceptance.** The coverage configuration names a floor per layer; the acceptance record shows each layer at or above its floor, or an exemption with a reason.

### RQ-03 — Execute the unverified surfaces (closes F-11)
**Statement.** The flow, integration, image-build, and cluster verification surfaces SHALL be executed on the supported runtime against live dependencies, and their results SHALL be recorded.
**Rationale.** Four surfaces were recorded as unverified for want of infrastructure; they are the remaining distance to an all-complete verification manifest.
**Target.** `spec/lifecycle/testing-architecture-qa.md` and `spec/lifecycle/ci-cd-pipeline.md`.
**Acceptance.** Each surface has a recorded run and verdict; none remains marked unverified.

### RQ-04 — Reconcile revision stamps (closes F-07)
**Statement.** The corpus revision SHALL be expressed consistently across the manifest, the change log, and the engine documents, or an explicit mapping SHALL be recorded where the figures legitimately differ.
**Rationale.** A reader must be able to state the corpus revision without ambiguity.
**Target.** `status/counts.json` and `upgradation/change-log.md`.
**Acceptance.** One declared corpus revision resolves to a single, documented mapping.

### RQ-05 — Normalise verification anchors (closes F-08)
**Statement.** Every verification document SHALL state the commit it was verified against, and the commits cited SHALL be reconciled so that no two documents disagree about the same claim.
**Rationale.** "Verified against commit X" is only useful when X is unambiguous.
**Target.** `status/code-reconciliation.json` and `blueprint/system-map.md`.
**Acceptance.** Each verification claim carries one commit; the cited commits reconcile across documents.

### RQ-06 — Reconcile the contract gap list (closes F-09)
**Statement.** The module-manifest contract's code-gap list and the reconciliation ledger SHALL tell one story — either the gaps are closed and removed, or they are re-affirmed as accepted with a recorded resolution.
**Rationale.** Two documents describing the same contract must not disagree about whether the code matches it.
**Target.** `contracts/module-manifest.schema.json` and `status/code-reconciliation.json`.
**Acceptance.** The contract carries no unaddressed gap that the reconciliation ledger reports as matched, and vice versa.

### RQ-07 — Populate the change log (closes F-12)
**Statement.** The change log's scope-and-method, master-version, defect-lifecycle, and open-items sections SHALL be populated to the standard its own entry template defines.
**Rationale.** The historical record is a first-class deliverable; empty sections should not read as complete.
**Target.** `upgradation/change-log.md`.
**Acceptance.** The four sections carry real entries; the population status is updated to reflect completion.

### RQ-08 — Populate the repository status file (closes F-13)
**Statement.** The repository status file SHALL state the current repository state and the module delivery boundary.
**Rationale.** The status file is a first-entry document; an empty template misleads.
**Target.** the repository status file.
**Acceptance.** The file states what the repository is and how modules reach runtime.

### RQ-09 — Keep deferred items explicitly out of scope (guards F-15)
**Statement.** Deferred capabilities and deferred greenfield items SHALL remain labelled deferred and MUST NOT be described as shipped anywhere in the corpus.
**Rationale.** Deferred work is not smuggled work.
**Target.** `status/capabilities.json` and `spec/reference/appendix-implementation-status-audit-register.md`.
**Acceptance.** Every deferred item remains labelled deferred; no shipped claim attaches to one.

### RQ-10 — Broaden counts enforcement (closes F-17)
**Statement.** The counts gate SHALL verify every count key that has a verifiable source, not only half of them.
**Rationale.** A count never checked against its source is not enforced.
**Target.** `status/counts.json`.
**Acceptance.** Every count key with a source is cross-checked by the gate.

## 8. Acceptance Criteria & Definition of Done

This request is done when all of the following hold:

- Every reader-facing artifact is human-attested or explicitly exempted with a reason (RQ-01).
- Coverage is enforced per layer; every layer meets its floor or carries a recorded exemption (RQ-02).
- No verification surface remains unexecuted (RQ-03).
- The corpus revision and every verification anchor are unambiguous (RQ-04, RQ-05).
- The contract and the reconciliation ledger agree (RQ-06).
- The change log and the repository status file are populated (RQ-07, RQ-08).
- Every deferred item is still labelled deferred (RQ-09).
- Every count with a source is gate-verified (RQ-10).
- The full gate suite passes with the new record present, and the new record itself passes the UR gate.

## 10. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Human review stalls, leaving the pass half-done | phase the work and close the highest-traffic documents first (see Phasing) |
| A revision or anchor change introduces a new inconsistency | the gates run on every change and fail on drift |
| Coverage tests are authored to hit the number rather than the behaviour | require each new test to assert specified behaviour, per the test architecture |
| The gap-list reconciliation silently drops a real gap | re-affirm each gap as accepted or closed with a resolution field, never by deletion alone |
| Scope creep into feature work | scope is fixed to documentation and ledgers; deferred items are excluded |

---

## 11. Phasing

- **P0 — attestation groundwork.** Start human review on `blueprint/system-map.md`, `spec/request-layer/`, and `spec/modules/`; reconcile revision stamps and verification anchors (RQ-01, RQ-04, RQ-05).
- **P1 — enforcement and evidence.** Per-layer coverage and the unverified surfaces (RQ-02, RQ-03); contract gap reconciliation and counts enforcement (RQ-06, RQ-10).
- **P2 — record completion.** Populate the change log and the repository status file; confirm deferred labelling (RQ-07, RQ-08, RQ-09).

Each phase closes only when the gate suite is green with its changes present.

## Appendix A — Record Shape (reusable)

A conforming UR record, per the register, carries:

1. Complete front-matter (`id`, `title`, `kind: ur`, `status`, `version`), so the UR gate discovers it dynamically.
2. An identity and provenance block naming the requester, the date, the related records, and the freeze status.
3. A purpose and a problem statement.
4. An explicit scope, with in-scope and out-of-scope lists.
5. An evidence base mapping each request to a finding.
6. Numbered requested changes, each with statement, rationale, target, and acceptance.
7. An impact matrix, acceptance criteria, and a traceability table.
8. Risks, phasing, and open questions.
9. A change-log record shape.

A new UR is a one-file operation: dropping `upgradation/UR-*.md` in is sufficient; nothing else needs to change.
