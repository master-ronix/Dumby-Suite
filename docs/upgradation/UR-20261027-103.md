# UR-20261027-103 — Citation Integrity

**Identity:** UR-20261027-103 · **Version:** 1.0 · **Date:** 27/10/2026 · **Requester:** the 2026-10-27 corpus audit

**Freeze status:** **Open — raised 2026-10-27.** It edits no frozen record and moves no file.

**Basis.** The 29-gate suite is green at `feature/dev` @ `0d749a9f`. A separate path sweep — written for
this audit because no gate validates a backticked path outside a UR record — found **263** unresolvable
corpus-prefixed citations under `docs/`: 66 in the live corpus, 167 under `archive/`, 30 under `_inbox/`.
The 66 live ones, plus three non-path defects found by reading, are the thirteen findings this record
closes. Nine of them share one cause: the retirement of the record tiers in commit `28d568a` repointed the
gates, the ledgers and the generated tree, but not the prose.

The work is small, mechanical and fully specified below. It touches documentation only. No code changes.

---

## 1. Objective and rationale

**Objective.** After this record lands, every corpus path the live documentation cites resolves, every
"verify against the code" block names an artefact that exists, and every statement the corpus makes about
its own currency matches its own contents.

**Rationale.** These are not cosmetic defects. Five of the thirteen are `high`, and in each the reader is
sent somewhere that does not exist: the audit-provenance paragraph in `docs/README.md` names four request
records by a retired path; the ADR series is cited through a directory that was moved; a normative MUST
rule attributes its own enforcement to a script that is not in the repository; a security section's
verification block cites a regression test that was never written; and four lifecycle specs send the
reader to `apps/main/.github/`, a tree that does not exist — while the same paragraph states the rule that
makes that path wrong.

The cost of leaving it undone is that the corpus's provenance claims stop being checkable. The corpus's own
stated policy is that its gates prove structural consistency, not truth; a citation that does not resolve is
exactly the class of error that policy leaves to a reader, and a reader cannot catch 66 of them.

## 2. Scope

**In scope.** Files under `docs/`: the live corpus (`README.md`, `_generated/`, `spec/`, `orchestration/`,
`documentation/`, `upgradation/`, `scripts/docs/` comments) and the status ledgers under `docs/status/`.

**Explicitly out of scope.**

- **`docs/archive/**` and `docs/_inbox/**`.** Frozen records. `docs/archive/v4.5.1/README.md` declares the
  tree "Frozen. NOT normative." and `archive/v5.0.0-records/upgradation/README.md` forbids modifying the
  source records. Their 197 retired-path citations are finding DOC-012, deferred by policy, not by
  oversight.
- **Moving files back.** The retirement in `28d568a` is deliberate; this record repoints citations, it does
  not undo the archive.
- **The shipped application.** No file under `apps/main/` or `packages/` is edited. Where a citation names
  an artefact that does not exist in the code, the record says which way to resolve it (add the artefact,
  or restate the claim and log the gap) and leaves the decision recorded rather than guessed.
- **`review_state` / `last_verified_commit`.** These stay `pending-evidence` / `null`. Populating them is
  human attestation and is not claimed by this record.

**Branch boundary.** All work happens on `feature/dev`. No commit, merge or push touches `main`, `master`,
`release` or any other branch.

## 4. Implementation plan

Dependency order, not file order. Each step is one atomic commit; the commit body names the finding IDs it
closes.

1. **Shared references first — DOC-001, DOC-002, DOC-003.** These three are the retired-path class and touch
   the widest set of files, including `docs/README.md`, the generated index, the orientation specs and the
   change-log. Doing them first means the later, narrower edits are made against final text.
2. **The gate comment — DOC-001 part 2.** `check-corpus-boundary.mjs` comments only. Kept in step 1's commit
   or its own; either is fine, but the gate must still pass afterwards.
3. **The provenance fields — DOC-004, DOC-005.** `status/audit-age.json` and `docs/README.md:67`. Depends on
   step 1 only because both files are also touched there; sequence the commits so `docs/README.md` is not
   edited twice for unrelated reasons.
4. **The verify-blocks — DOC-006, DOC-009.** Path corrections in `spec/lifecycle/`,
   `spec/architecture/`, `spec/reference/`. Independent of steps 1–3.
5. **The two missing artefacts — DOC-007, DOC-008.** These are the only items that may require a decision
   (implement versus record the gap). Do them after the pure path fixes, so the diff that changes an
   assertion is separate from the diff that fixes a path.
6. **The statements about currency and extent — DOC-011, DOC-013.** Ledger and definition prose.
7. **The structural link — DOC-010.** Last, because if it adds files it changes `_generated/tree.md` and
   `status/counts.json`, and those must be regenerated against the final tree.
8. **The record — this run's change-log entry and activation line**, appended once every step above is
   committed and the gate suite is green.

Order rationale: steps 1–3 fix provenance surfaces that other documents quote; step 4 is mechanical; step 5
is the only step with a decision in it; step 7 is the only step that changes the tree.

  record; the record only forbids leaving the claim standing against a phantom file.

### Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| A repointed path is itself wrong, so the citation moves from one dead path to another | low | medium | ACC-2 requires the sweep to report zero; each step's acceptance criterion requires `ls` to resolve the named path |

### Rollback

- Every step is one atomic commit with a finding-ID body. `git revert <sha>` undoes exactly one step.
- No step deletes a file. The largest structural change is adding two index pages (DOC-010), undone by
  `git revert`.
- No force-push, no rebase of published history, no reset over work this run did not author, and no
  protected-branch change. Nothing here is irreversible.
- If the gate suite goes red and the cause is not obvious within one attempt, stop and report the failing
