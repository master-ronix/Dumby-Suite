## 1. The directive (verbatim, formalised)

> "Only residual for browse module, Discover button is still not in-line with title."

- **C1 — Discover in-line with the title.** The **Discover** control must sit on the **same line as
  the content title** (`Explore new modules to install`), not on a row above it.

---

## 2. Status assessment (against `feature/dev` @ `e1d72fb`)

| Clause | Status before | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| C1 Discover in-line | **Violated** | Discover was a `row` block above a separate `text` title block |

---

## 3. Requested changes

- **R1 (C1).** The browse title becomes a **Section** whose **accessory is the Discover control**;
  the standalone control row and the separate title text block are removed. A Section renders its
  single accessory on the right of the text line, so Discover now sits **in-line with the title**.
