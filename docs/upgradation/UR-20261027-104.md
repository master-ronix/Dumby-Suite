# UR-20261027-104 — Enforcement Pass: Contract Truth and Gate Scope

**Identity:** UR-20261027-104 · **Version:** 1.0 · **Date:** 27/10/2026 · **Requester:** the 2026-10-27 corpus enforcement audit

**Freeze status:** **Open — raised 2026-10-27.** It edits no frozen record and moves no file. It adds no
directory and retires no tier.

**Basis.** The 29-gate suite is green at `feature/dev` @ `c6c2c82a`, and CI on that commit is green across
all three jobs. Both facts were verified this run, not assumed. They are also the reason this record exists:
a green suite and a green pipeline are the two things that make an unverified surface look verified. Reading
the tree against the code, and reading the gates against the tree, found eight defects that no gate is
positioned to catch — the largest being a contract that documents eleven endpoints the server does not
serve, and six gates that have been silently scanning nothing since the ADR series moved in `28d568a`.

Eight findings, `DOC-017` … `DOC-024`. Three are `high`, four `medium`, one `low`. Seven are fixed in this
record's own run; one (`DOC-022`) narrows a claim and records the gap it cannot close without a code change.

---

## 1. Objective and rationale

**Objective.** After this record lands: every path in the OpenAPI contract is a path the server mounts;
every gate's scope list names a directory that exists; the audit-age ledger states the corpus's audit age and
CI position as they actually are at HEAD; and the two registers that exist to say what happened to a request
and what the corpus verified say so for the most recent work.

**Rationale.** Four of the eight are the same shape — a statement that was true when written, left standing
after the thing it described moved. The ADR series moved and six gates kept their old scope, so the corpus's
only machine verification has been proving nothing about the 16 records it calls normative, while printing
`OK`. The CI billing block cleared and the red signal stayed in the ledger, so the machine-readable audit-age
record tells a maintainer that four named tests are failing at Gate 4 when they are green. A request
disposition register stops two rows short of the corpus, so the newest record — the one still in the tree —
looks undisposed.

The OpenAPI defect is the most expensive. `contracts/openapi.yaml` calls itself the single source for the
REST/webhook/MDIS surface and describes a surface that does not exist: eleven of its sixteen paths have no
route in the repository, and the MDIS, Twill, operational, Power Engine, OAuth and deploy families that the
server does mount are absent from it. The corpus's own policy — stated in `README.md` — is that such a
divergence is recorded in `status/code-reconciliation.json` and never smoothed over; it was not recorded.

None of these are caught by a gate today, and that is the point of the record: a corpus that is
gate-verified and CI-verified still needs someone to check that the gates are looking at the right files and
that the contracts are describing the right system.

### 3.1 DOC-017 — the contract and the server disagree

- **Defect.** `contracts/openapi.yaml:5` declares itself the "Single source for REST/webhook/MDIS
  endpoints". Eleven of its sixteen paths are not mounted anywhere in the repository, and the families the
  server does mount are absent from it.
- **Location.** `contracts/openapi.yaml:12` (`/info`), `:14-20` (`/api/modules/{name}/config|state|{action}`),
  `:21-26` (`/api/swastik/register|modules/{slug}/upload|modules/{slug}/status`), `:27` (`/admin/operators`),
  `:31-36` (`/channels/`, `/guilds/`, `/interactions/`). The parallel path list in
  `documentation/api/endpoints.md:26-40` carries the same `/info` and `/api/modules/*` paths, and
  `spec/reference/rest-api-webhooks-mdis-reference.md:102-108` carries the `/api/swastik/*` trio.
- **Evidence.** The served surface, enumerated from the registrations:
  `app.(get|post|put|patch|delete)('…')` across `apps/main/src` and `packages/*/src` yields **67** unique
  paths, including `/api/info`, `/mdis/catalog`, `/mdis/guilds/:guildId/{install,installed,uninstall}`,
  `/mdis/modules/:slug`, `/operational/{runbooks,status}`,
  `/swastik/{developers/me,developers/register,modules/upload,modules/:slug/builds/:buildId,status}`,
  `/twill/{personas,registrars}`, `/admin/config`, `/admin/system/maintenance-mode`, 32
  `/admin/power-engine/*` routes, `/internal/{deploy,reload}`, `/auth/{authorize,callback}`,
  `/oauth/callback`, `/dashboard`, `/openapi.yaml`, the two `/assets/*` banners, and the three `/health/*`
  probes. A repo-wide search for the contract's declared strings — `admin/operators`, `api/modules`,
  `channels/`, `guilds/`, `interactions/` — finds no route registration. `apps/main/src/http/server.ts:79-116`
  shows what is mounted: `registerInfoRoutes`, `registerHealthRoutes`, `registerMetricsRoutes`,
  `registerAdminRoutes`, `registerMdisRoutes`, `registerOperationalRoutes`, `registerOAuthRoutes`,
  `registerTwillPersonaRoutes`, `registerModuleDeployRoutes`. `status/code-reconciliation.json` carries 35
  facts (34 `match`, 1 `code-gap` = `REC-035`) and records none of this, against `README.md`'s stated policy
  that divergence "is recorded in `status/code-reconciliation.json` — never smoothed over".
- **Approach.** Enumerate the shipped routes from the registrations and rewrite the contract's `paths:`
  block to them, one line per path and method, with the auth context the server actually applies
  (`apps/main/src/http/server.ts:79-91` protects `/admin/*`, `/mdis/*`, `/operational/*`, `/twill/*` and
  `/internal/*` with `INTERNAL_API_KEY`). Correct the path lists in `documentation/api/endpoints.md` to the
  same surface. The specification keeps its own layout — it is the authority, and the standing rule in
  `status/code-reconciliation.json` is "the specification is authority; where the code is narrower, the code
  is the gap to close" — so the spec's path blocks are **not** rewritten; instead three `code-gap` rows are
  added to `status/code-reconciliation.json` (`REC-036`, `REC-037`, `REC-038`) and the contract carries an
  `x-spec-divergence` block naming each specified-versus-shipped shape, so neither surface has to lie about
  the other.
- **Acceptance criteria.**
  1. Every `paths:` entry in `contracts/openapi.yaml` matches a route registration: a comparison script
     reports 67/67, zero contract-only and zero code-only.
  2. `documentation/api/endpoints.md` names only paths that appear in that same enumeration.
  3. `status/code-reconciliation.json` carries `REC-036`, `REC-037` and `REC-038` as `code-gap`, covering
     the info/MDIS/Swastik layout, the `/api/pep/{functionName}` control plane, and `/channels/`,
     `/guilds/`, `/interactions/` and `/admin/operators`, and `node scripts/docs/check-code-reconciliation.mjs`
     passes.
  4. The contract's `info.description` no longer claims a scope it does not cover, and names the
     reconciliation rows that carry the divergence.

## 4. Implementation plan

Dependency order, not file order. Each step is one atomic commit; the commit body names the finding IDs it
closes.

1. **Gate scope — `DOC-018`, `DOC-019`.** Both are gate scripts and both change what the suite looks at.
   Doing them first means every later step is verified by a suite that is actually scanning the corpus. Run
   all 29 gates immediately after.
2. **The contract — `DOC-017`.** The enumeration, the rewritten `paths:` block, the corrected
   documentation path list, and the three `code-gap` rows. Independent of step 1 in content, but verified
   by it.
3. **The narrowed claim — `DOC-022`.** Separate commit: it changes an assertion, and an assertion change
   should be reviewable apart from a path change.
4. **The provenance fields — `DOC-020`, `DOC-021`.** One commit for `status/audit-age.json` and the
   `README.md` paragraph, since both surfaces must name the same audit and the same CI position.
5. **The headings — `DOC-023`.** `spec/orientation/definitions.md` only; re-run the three anchor/coverage
   gates.
6. **The register — `DOC-024`.** Two rows in `upgradation/change-log.md` §5.1.
7. **The tree — this record.** Adding `upgradation/UR-20261027-104.md` changes the generated tree, so
   `_generated/tree.md` and the `tree_entries` / `tree_files` keys in `status/counts.json` are updated in
   this step, verified by `check-generated` and `check-counts`.
8. **The record of the run —** the activation line and the version entry in `upgradation/change-log.md`,
   appended once every step above is committed and the suite is green.

Order rationale: steps 1–2 fix what the corpus verifies and what it claims; steps 3–6 are narrow statements
about currency, extent and disposition; step 7 is the only step that changes the tree, and step 8 must be
last because it records the result of the others.

## 6. Dependencies, risks and rollback

### Dependencies

- **`feature/dev` write access.** Verified in this run: the clone, the commits and the push go through the
  connector-injected credential on `https://github.com/`. If the write route fails, the record is delivered
  as a file and commits are reported as not run.
- **A readable `apps/main/src` tree.** `DOC-017`'s enumeration is derived from it. If the tree is not
  readable the contract cannot be corrected and the finding stays open with the gap recorded.
- **No upstream service.** Documentation-only: no build, no deploy, no database.

### Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| The rewritten contract omits a route registered by a form the enumeration misses | medium | high | enumerate all four registration forms (`app.<verb>('…')`, multi-line, `app.route({url})`, module-mounted prefixes), then diff both directions and require zero on each side; ACC-2 is that diff |
| Widening the gates' scope surfaces a violation in an ADR that was never scanned | medium | medium | the six gates were run against the widened scope before this record was written: all six pass and the counts rise by exactly 16 |
| A rewritten contract path is itself wrong, moving the reader from one dead path to another | low | medium | every path in the contract comes from a shown enumeration, and ACC-2 fails on any drift |
| Editing `spec/**` changes a heading the coverage ledger maps | low | high | `DOC-023` renames no heading text a ledger row targets — it retitles two `definitions.md` sections whose rows are verified by `check-coverage` and `check-ledger-content` in the same step |
| `DOC-022`'s narrowing looks like weakening a requirement | medium | medium | the invariant survives in the `code-gap` row; only the claim that a test enforces it changes |
| The change-log gains a line that restates a count a side-document gate polices | low | medium | the entry states no gate count and no record count; `check-side-docs` runs before the commit |

### Rollback

- Every step is one atomic commit with a finding-ID body. `git revert <sha>` undoes exactly one step.
- No step deletes a file. The largest change is the contract's `paths:` block, undone by `git revert`.
- No force-push, no rebase of published history, no reset over work this run did not author, and no
  protected-branch change. Nothing here is irreversible.
- If the suite goes red and the cause is not obvious within one attempt, stop and report the failing gate
  with its output rather than reverting blindly.
