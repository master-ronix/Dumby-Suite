# ADR-007: Detection Honesty — Measured Rates, Not Absolute Claims

**Status:** Accepted — 18/09/2026
**Deciders:** Dumby Core Engineering — Quixotic Mnemonics
**Supersedes:** The "zero false positives / zero false negatives" phrasing as previously used across the detection-surface sections.
**Governing sections:** blueprint.md 5.0.0 §13.6, §13.8, §16.4, §21, §37, §43

## Context

The 4.x corpus claims "zero false positives / zero false negatives" for its detection
surfaces. A literal zero is not verifiable: it cannot be demonstrated by any test, it
invites the exact spec-code drift the honest-audit doctrine exists to expose, and — worst
case — it gets "fixed" by renaming the claim while changing nothing underneath. The same
honesty gap applies to "strongest possible security": superlatives without measurements
are unfalsifiable, and unfalsifiable claims in a contract document are defects.

The competing options were:

1. Keep the absolute claims and add caveats around them.
2. Soften the wording only ("near-zero", "minimal") without measurement behind it.
3. Reframe detection as a measured property with real instrumentation behind the claim.

## Decision

Detection surfaces are described as what they are: **measured, tunable, ground-truthed,
and CI-tracked, with rates driven toward the floor.** No detection surface is described
with an absolute zero claim. Every rate statement in the corpus carries its measurement —
the ground-truth corpus it is measured against and the CI job that tracks it. The same
invariant applies to every superlative claim in the shipped docs: no "zero false…", no
"strongest possible…" appears without its measured form beside it.

## Consequences

- **Maintainers** must back detection claims with a ground-truth corpus per surface and a
  CI job that reports the rates; a claim without its measurement cannot merge.
- **QA** runs a corpus-wide search for unmeasured superlatives ("zero false",
  "strongest possible") and reviews every hit for its measured form — this check is part
  of the acceptance definition of the re-baseline.
- **Operators** see real rates and the tuning levers that move them, rather than an
  assurance that cannot be checked.
- **The honest-audit doctrine extends from code to claims about code:** per-section status
  badges already state implementation truth; this ADR makes the same demand of qualitative
  performance claims.
- **Invariant:** "driven toward the floor" is an asymptote honestly stated — the docs
  never assert arrival.
