# ADR-008: Twill Free-Charter Sunset

**Status:** Accepted — 18/09/2026
**Deciders:** Dumby Core Engineering — Quixotic Mnemonics
**Supersedes:** None — records an explicit end condition for a default that previously had none.
**Governing sections:** blueprint.md 5.0.0 §35, §6.6, §18.5.1, §7.5, §7.6.1, §22.3, §32, §44

## Context

Twill — the persona subsystem formally renamed from "Twixter" — is a bring-your-own-identity
model: a guild administrator supplies their own bot token and application, Dumby hosts the
instance, and one persona is bound to exactly one server. The default charter under which
personas run is free: no fee, metering, or billing attaches to hosting a persona instance.

An open-ended free default accrues a specific risk the register names: unbounded free
usage, hosting cost without a recapture path, and a de facto promise the program never
ratified. The competing options were:

1. Keep the free charter open-ended and decide later.
2. Begin monetization wiring now, alongside the v5.0 program.
3. Keep the free charter but give it an explicit, recorded end condition.

Option 2 is out of scope by the program's own exclusion rule (monetization wiring is
excluded from this re-baseline); option 1 repeats the pattern this program exists to end —
decisions presented as settled when they were never made.

## Decision

The free default charter for Twill personas carries an explicit end condition: a
recorded sunset. Personas remain free-chartered until the sunset takes effect. Monetization
wiring — payments, billing, metering, pricing — stays out of scope of the v5.0 program
and is not implied, begun, or pre-wired by this ADR. When the program eventually takes up
monetization, it arrives as new request records under its own program, not as an edit to
this one.

## Consequences

- **Guild administrators** hosting persona instances operate under a charter that states
  its own sunset condition; the terms they accept are the terms documented.
- **Maintainers** must not merge monetization surfaces into the persona subsystem during
  the v5.0 program — the exclusion is standing, and deferred work is not smuggled work.
- **concept.md and master-prompt.md** reflect the Twill naming and the single-server
  binding; the charter's sunset condition is part of the persona documentation.
- **Operators** see hosting load for what it is: free until sunset, with the end condition
  recorded here rather than discovered at renewal time.
- **Invariant:** one persona = one server; free until the recorded sunset; no monetization
  before a future program says so.
